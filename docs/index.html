Music

It listens with you

Whatever is playing, in any app, shows up in the notch by itself.

Now playing

Song, artist, and cover, with play, pause, and skip. Click or drag the progress bar to jump.

Live lyrics

The line being sung appears in the notch and in the closed pill, which grows and shrinks to fit it.

Sing-along fill

The current line fills with light as it is sung. Hebrew and Arabic fill from the right.

Lyrics page

The whole song in a scrolling column. Click any line to jump there, or scroll by hand with the wheel.

Cinema

Lyrics on the whole screen: big cover, big words, and clouds in the cover's colors drifting behind.

Interlude dots

Three dots fill one by one through the intro and the breaks between verses.

Song colors

The notch glows in the color of the album cover, and melts smoothly into the next song's.

Bars on the beat

Three little bars follow the bass, the mids, and the treble of what you actually hear.

Get things done

Small tools, always close

Focus timer

Work in focused stretches with breaks in between, and see how many minutes you focused today.

Countdown and stopwatch

A plain timer with quick +1 and +5 minute buttons, and a stopwatch, in the same tab.

To-do

Type a task, press Enter, tick it off. The mascot cheers when you finish one.

Calendar with reminders

Pick a day, add an event and a time. Piztki reminds you ten minutes before and when it starts.

Water

Count your glasses toward a daily goal, with a gentle nudge when it's time to drink.

Shelf

Drag files onto the notch to park them, then drag them out wherever you need them.

Notes

A few quick notes, right in the notch. They save by themselves.

Shortcuts

Pin the apps, files, and folders you open most and launch them with one click.

Sound mixer

A volume slider for every app, so the game can be quiet while the music stays loud.

Screenshot

Drag over part of the screen. The picture lands on the shelf and is copied too.

Downloads

See a file while your browser downloads it, and get told the moment it's done.

Mic and camera dots

A small dot lights up while an app is using your microphone or camera. One click mutes the mic.

Focus chart

How long you focused on each of the last seven days.

Search box

Open apps and files, do sums, and convert units and money from one box.

Quick timer

Press the notch, pull down, and let go on 5, 10, or 25 minutes.

Notifications

A new message from any app shows in the notch for a few seconds.

Color picker

Click any pixel on the screen and its color code is copied.

Stretch reminders

A friendly nudge to stand up after an hour at the screen.

Themes

Midnight, Neon, Glass, or Pastel. One click changes the whole look.

Game summary

Fortnite: your place, eliminations, damage, and accuracy, back in the lobby. Roblox: the game, server location, players, and ping when you join, and how long you played.

Game mode

A tiny strip with the time, CPU, GPU, and memory while you play.

Clipboard

The last few things you copied, one click away. Kept in memory and forgotten when you quit.

At a glance

Know without looking it up

Home

One card per feature: the song, the timer, your next task, your next event, all on one page.

Weather

The temperature in your city, under the clock. Type the city or let Piztki find it.

World clocks and the moon

Two cities of your choice, tonight's moon phase, and a countdown to a day you're waiting for.

System

Processor, memory, and network speed, when you want to know why things feel slow.

Volume popup

Change the volume and the pill shows the level for a moment.

Caps Lock and language

A quick note when Caps Lock flips, and which keyboard language you just switched to.

Battery and internet

Plugged in, unplugged, running low, connection lost, back online.

The mascot

A little friend lives in there

Piztki is a night-sky cube with floating hands. It blinks, follows your pointer with its eyes, waves when the notch opens, and dances when music plays. Try it:

Hat

Moods

Happy after a win, sleepy late at night, a little sad when the battery runs low.

Textures

Stars, carbon, waves, honeycomb, or plain. Five looks for the cube.

Poke it

Make it yours

It fits the way you work

Switch anything off

Every feature has its own switch. Turn one off and its tab disappears.

Any screen

Got two monitors? Choose which one the notch lives on.

Attached or floating

Joined to the top edge of the screen, or a pill floating just below it.

Three sizes and a minimal mode

Small, medium, or large, and a tiny pill when nothing is playing.

Hover or click

Open it by touching it with the pointer, or only when you click.

Out of the way

It slides off the screen for full-screen games and videos, and comes back after.

Light

About 45 MB of memory. Hidden parts stop animating, so it stays quiet in the background.

Install

Three steps, nothing to set up

Download

Get the zip and unzip it anywhere you like.

Run it

Open Piztki.exe. Nothing else needs to be installed.

Say yes to Windows

The first time, Windows may say it "protected your PC", as it does for any app outside a store. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

To quit, right-click the Piztki icon next to the clock. To start it with your PC, turn on "Start with Windows" in its settings.

Privacy

Your stuff stays on your PC

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your tasks, events, and settings are saved only on your own computer. A few features use free outside services (lyrics, weather, finding your city). Each sends only what it needs, and every one can be switched off. Read the full privacy policy.
